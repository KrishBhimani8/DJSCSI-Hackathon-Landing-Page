Overview

What is
HACKDJSCE

HACKDJSCE is the flagship 24-hour hackathon organized by the Computer Society of India, DJ Sanghvi College of Engineering (DJSCE) chapter. Teams choose from a set of real-world problem statements across multiple tracks and build a working solution within the time limit.

Open to students from any college, of any experience level. Mentors are available throughout to help teams debug, scope, and ship.

Pick Your Track

Problem
Statements

Every team selects one problem statement at registration or during kickoff. Each track is judged on the same criteria: impact, execution, and technical depth.

Track 01 · Campus Life

Fix everyday college friction

Build a tool that solves a real problem for students — timetable clashes, canteen queues, lost-and-found, or club coordination.

Track 02 · FinTech

Make money management simple

Design a solution for budgeting, split expenses, micro-savings, or fraud detection for students or small merchants.

Track 03 · HealthTech

Improve access to care

Build something that helps people track health, find care faster, or manage chronic conditions with better data.

Track 04 · Sustainability

Reduce waste, track impact

Create tools that help individuals or institutions measure and cut down their environmental footprint.

Track 05 · EdTech

Make learning stick

Build a tool that helps students learn, revise, or collaborate more effectively than existing options.

Track 06 · Open Innovation

Bring your own problem

No fixed brief — pitch and build anything, as long as you can defend the problem it solves.

Guidelines

Rules & Eligibility

Open to all college students

Any recognized institution, not just DJSCE. Valid student ID required at check-in.

Teams of 2 to 4

Solo entries and teams above 4 are not accepted.

Build during the event window only

Code must be written within the 24 hours. Libraries and design assets are fine; pre-built projects are not.

AI-assisted building is welcome

Allowed and encouraged, as long as your team can explain the architecture behind it.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need a team before I register?

No — register solo and form a team at kickoff, or bring one already formed.

Is there a registration fee?

No, it's free for all registered students. Meals and workspace are provided.

Can I switch problem statements later?

You can change tracks up until kickoff briefing ends at 11:00 AM on Day 1.

Is DJSCE the only eligible college?

No, students from any college can register and attend.
